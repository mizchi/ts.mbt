# Feedback cases: tsc's own report is the behaviour in question, and the
# checker deliberately does not reproduce it.
declareFieldReadInDerivedInitializer:*  mtsc folds `declare` into `!`, so a declare field is never "used before initialization"; tsc's TS2729 here is the questioned behaviour
nullishCoalescingVoidLeftOperand:*      mtsc has no TS2871, and skips `void 0 ?? y` for TS2869 on purpose: tsc's TS2869 there is the questioned behaviour
